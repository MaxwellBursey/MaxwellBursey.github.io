Snow-clearing device for elderly users

An accessibility-focused snow-clearing device designed for elderly users, modelled in 3D.

Role
Design partner (team of two)
Timeline
2023
Tools
Onshape · Microsoft Word
Deliverables
Detailed 3D model · comprehensive design report

The brief

Snow clearing is physically demanding and a genuine hazard for elderly people - falls and overexertion injuries spike every winter in Newfoundland. Working with a partner, I designed a snow-clearing device specifically around the capabilities and limits of elderly users, treating accessibility as the primary design constraint rather than an afterthought.

Design work

We developed a detailed 3D model in Onshape, working through mechanical design principles with constant attention to usability and durability: how the device is gripped, how much force operation requires, how it stands up to repeated cold-weather use. The CAD model went through iterations as usability considerations pushed back on the initial mechanical concept.

Documentation

The project concluded with a comprehensive design report covering the design rationale, the decisions made at each stage, and final specifications - practice in the kind of formal design documentation that engineering work runs on.
